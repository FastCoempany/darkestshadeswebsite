# Model bibles

The seven, picked from round 1 (`README.md`). Each bible is one fixed paragraph, pasted word for word into every prompt for that person, so the same person comes back shot after shot. The frames will cover the eyes and brows, the most identity-defining part of a face, so the bibles lean on what stays visible: head shape, hair, skin, cheekbones, nose, lips, jaw, neck and build.

Generation never draws glasses. The real frame is composited afterwards (`docs/brief/07-photography.md`, track 2).

| Slot | Picked | Figma node | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | W1A | `1:2` | The blunt bob and fringe stay unmistakable with the eyes covered, and the brow bar sits under the fringe. |
| W2 | W2A | `2:2` | Close-cropped head and sculpted cheekbones: the strongest silhouette in the round, ears and temples clear. |
| W3 | W3A | `3:2` | Hair pulled back tight, long face, no softness: the most severe of the pair, and a third distinct silhouette next to W1 and W2. |
| M1 | M1A | `4:2` | Gaunt, pale and angular, with a short crop: the leanest face in the round. |
| M2 | M2B | `12:2` | Shaved head and a long, lean face; long rather than heavy. |
| M3 | M3A | `6:2` | Swept-back hair, stubble kept short, an unstyled presence; cleaner lines than the full beard. |
| NB | NBA | `7:2` | Long center-parted hair and strong bone structure read neither feminine nor masculine; widens the cast's range. |

## The bibles

**W1.** A 24-year-old Korean woman, slender with narrow shoulders and a long neck. Fair, smooth, matte skin with a cool undertone. Glossy blue-black hair in a blunt bob cut level with the jaw, a heavy straight fringe cut just above the brows, the hair falling straight over both ears. Long oval face, high flat cheekbones, a small straight nose with a low bridge, small full lips in their natural soft rose. No makeup, no jewelry. Still and unsmiling, mouth closed.

**W2.** A 29-year-old West African woman, lean and long-necked. Very dark brown skin with a warm undertone and a soft natural sheen on the cheekbones and forehead. Natural hair cropped very close, about a centimeter long, following the round shape of the head; ears fully visible. Long oval face, very high sculpted cheekbones, a defined jaw, a broad nose, full wide lips in their natural deep color. No makeup, no jewelry. Composed and serious, mouth closed.

**W3.** A 40-year-old Italian woman, lean, with a long neck. Olive skin with fine natural lines, no retouching. Very dark brown hair parted in the center and pulled back tightly into a low bun at the nape; ears visible. Long angular face, high cheekbones, a long straight nose, thin lips, a defined jaw. No makeup, no jewelry. Severe and calm, mouth closed.

**M1.** A 26-year-old Northern European man, very lean with a long neck and narrow shoulders. Very pale skin with a cool pink undertone. Ash-blond hair in a short crop, close at the sides, a little longer at the front; ears that stand slightly out from the head. Gaunt angular face, hollow cheeks, a very sharp jaw, a long straight nose, thin lips. Clean-shaven. Still and unsmiling, mouth closed.

**M2.** A 34-year-old Nigerian man, lean and long-limbed. Deep dark brown skin with a soft sheen. Cleanly shaved head; ears visible. Long lean face, high cheekbones, a strong jaw, a broad nose, full lips. Clean-shaven. Calm and intent, mouth closed.

**M3.** A 38-year-old South Asian man, lean. Medium brown skin with a warm undertone. Thick black hair swept back off the forehead, short at the sides; ears visible. Long face, defined cheekbones, a straight prominent nose, a close stubble of two or three days along the jaw and upper lip. Unstyled, serious, mouth closed.

**NB.** A 27-year-old Mexican Indigenous non-binary person, slim. Warm light-brown skin, smooth. Straight black hair parted in the center, falling to the jaw and just past it, tucked behind the left ear on one side. Broad high cheekbones, a strong straight nose, full lips, a defined jaw; neither feminine nor masculine in styling. No makeup, no jewelry. Still and direct, mouth closed.

## Prompt template

```
[Scene block from the prompt pack: place, light, lens, framing.]
[Bible paragraph, word for word.]
Wardrobe: [uniform-like, no logos: black nylon, white shirting, fine knit, long coat].
Head [facing the camera, chin level | turned three-quarters to the left, chin level | in profile to the left], eyes open, looking just past the camera.
No glasses or sunglasses, nothing on or near the face, temples and ears clear of hands and objects.
Photograph, natural skin texture, no retouching. No text, no logos.
```

The head angle matches the cut-out it will wear (A1 front, A2 three-quarter, A3 side). The grade (grain, lifted blacks, warm whites) is applied after the composite, not in the prompt.
